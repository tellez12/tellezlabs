EventosLocales.net

Public product · Spanish and English · own domain, SEO and analytics

What's on in Málaga province, in one place. Every day a worker pulls events from theatres, clubs, town-hall agendas, Fever and Ticketmaster, works out which listings are the same show, groups a run of dates into one event, and tracks when tickets go on sale or sell out.

  • Cross-source de-duplication by venue, date and fuzzy title, plus a daily merge job
  • Server-rendered SEO heads, schema.org events and a sitemap for a React SPA
  • Alerts, venue and artist follows, calendar files and per-event traffic stats
Backend
Python 3 · FastAPI · SQLAlchemy 2 async · Alembic
Data
PostgreSQL 16 + PostGIS · Redis
Ingest
Source connectors · Playwright · worker
Frontend
React · Vite · TypeScript · nginx SSI
Ops

ChessHelper

Chess coach for chess.com and Lichess players · web + iPhone

Connect your chess.com or Lichess account and every game you finish gets analysed by Stockfish. ChessHelper labels each mistake (hanging piece, missed fork, endgame technique), shows where you lose games by phase, clock and opening, and turns your own blunders into puzzles.

  • Background engine worker analysing thousands of games per profile
  • Rule-based study plan and tilt detection, with no guesswork from the AI
  • Coaching reports written by Gemini from the engine's numbers, in four coach voices

Engine

GymHelper

Gym tracker · web, iOS and Android

A training log built around a real exercise library and a recommender that writes your plan from your goal, experience, schedule and equipment. It keeps what you planned apart from what you actually lifted, so progress, records and streaks reflect real sessions.

  • Deterministic recommender from curated movement patterns, no AI needed
  • Optional AI coach (Gemini) that answers from your own plan and history
  • Whoop recovery data and health-metric webhooks feed the dashboard

AI

Frontend

GuitarHelper

Practice companion for beginners · installable web app

The chords you know decide everything else: which songs you can play today, how hard each one is, which chord to learn next and what goes into tomorrow's practice plan. Songs come from the top charts or are imported from Ultimate Guitar and LaCuerda.

  • Next chord ranked by how many of your songs it unlocks
  • Tuner, metronome, strumming trainer and one-minute changes, all offline
  • Deliberately no AI: every suggestion is explainable from your chords
